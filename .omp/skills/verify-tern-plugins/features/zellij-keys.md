# Zellij floats and panes

The zellij-keys plugin gives Tern the Zellij pane actions that it has no command for: floating shells and tools, floats that are shown or hidden together in a cascade, floating and docking a pane, breaking a pane out to a tab, and moving the focus among floats. A focus on a tiled pane hides the floats of its tab. A float change does not animate, and the `reduce_motion` preference of the user comes back after it. `settings.json` also binds Tern's own actions to Zellij keys: the agent tab and the scroll and find keys of Ctrl+G S.

## Sub-features

- `zk-new-pane` splits a tiled pane to the right, or opens a floating shell from a float (Alt+N).
- `zk-float-tool` opens yazi, lazygit or btop in a float (F2, F3, F4), centered at 80% of the tab in width and in height, in place of the half-tab cascade place. A move or resize starts there, and Ctrl+G, R, = puts it at its cascade place.
- `zk-cascade` draws each shown float at half the tab in width and in height. The first is centered, and each later one sits 16 px to the right and 32 px lower, in open order. After 8 places the cascade starts at the top again and keeps going right. No "Show all" chip shows.
- `zk-corners` spreads the floats of a tiled pane over the four corner stacks, so Tern draws 15 of them instead of 4, and 16 when there are more and the Go to picker is closed. Tern hides the oldest extra floats, never the focused one.
- `zk-float-moves` moves (Ctrl+G, M, and H, J, K, L or an arrow) and resizes (Ctrl+G, R, and the same keys) the focused float by a tenth of the tab, and grows or shrinks it on all sides (Ctrl+G, R, + or -, and Alt +, Alt =, Alt -). The float stays inside the tab, and an edge within 5% of a tab edge snaps to 10 px from it. A float that lets go of its one snapped edge on an axis keeps its place on that axis: a narrower float at the right edge keeps its left edge. Ctrl+G, R, = puts it back at its cascade place. It keeps its spot through hide and show, a tab switch and `tern plugin reload`, until it closes or docks. In a tiled pane the same keys move the pane and the divider as before, and Alt + and Alt - do nothing.
- `zk-toggle-floats` opens a floating shell when the tab has no float, hides the floats from a float, and shows them all from a tiled pane with focus on the last used float (Alt+F). It does so in every tab, also in a new tab after a hide in another tab. The last used float survives `tern plugin reload`. Hidden floats keep Tern's native edge pills.
- `zk-hide-on-focus` hides the floats of a tab when focus goes to a tiled pane of that tab from a pane of the same tab, every time: also in a new tab, and when one float is shown and another is hidden. A switch to another tab or session and back hides nothing, also when a tiled pane had focus with the floats shown.
- `zk-close-float` sends focus to the last used float, else the float before the closed one, when the focused float closes. The last float sends focus to the tiled pane.
- `zk-picker` sends focus back to the float that opened the Go to picker on Esc. Each jump from the picker to another tiled pane hides the floats.
- `zk-toggle-float` floats the focused tiled pane, or docks the focused float (Ctrl+G, P, E).
- `zk-break` moves the focused pane to a new tab (Ctrl+G, T, B), or to the tab on the left or right (Ctrl+G, T, `[` or `]`).
- `zk-focus` moves the focus among the floats from a float, in open order and around from the last to the first (Alt+H and Alt+K back, Alt+L and Alt+J forward, and the arrows). From a tiled pane it moves to the tiled pane on that side, and Alt+H and Alt+L go to the tab on that side when there is no pane there. Alt+K and Alt+J never change the tab.
- `zk-agent-tab` opens a new tab with an agent block in the folder of the focused pane (Alt+Shift+T). The key runs Tern's own `new_agent_block`, and the copied `settings.json` sets `agent_placement` to `new_tab`, so Tern's `New agent block` in the palette opens a tab too.
- `zk-worktree-agent-tab` opens an agent tab in a new worktree at the remote default branch (Ctrl+Shift+T), while the clean main checkout keeps its current branch. Dirty main checkouts and ahead or diverged local default branches stop with an error toast. A failed fetch uses the cached remote branch; without it, the local default branch is the fallback.
- `zk-scroll` scrolls the focused terminal pane with Tern's actions on Ctrl+G, S: J and K one line, Ctrl+F or Page Down and Ctrl+B or Page Up one page, Ctrl+C to the bottom. With the find bar open (Ctrl+G, S, F), N goes to the next match toward the newest output and P to the previous one.
- `zk-motion` sets `reduce_motion` to `on` while a float changes, and back to the value before after 400 ms.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- The keys above, from `settings.json`. `keybinds.html` lists them under the Zellij mode of each.
- The command palette, by the command title: `New pane (floating from a floating pane)`, `New agent tab in a new worktree`, `Float yazi`, `Float lazygit`, `Float btop`, `Toggle floating panes`, `Float or dock the focused pane`, `Break pane to new tab`, `Break pane to the tab on the left`, `Break pane to the tab on the right`, `Focus the pane on the left` (and right, above, below), `Focus the pane or tab on the left` (and right), `Resize: narrower float or divider left` (and wider, shorter, taller), `Grow the floating pane`, `Shrink the floating pane`. Tern's own `New agent block` opens the agent tab.

## Driving it with tern-verify

Preconditions:

- Tern 0.9.7 (`c2f7563`) and omp 18.13.3 are the observed baseline. Start with one tiled pane and `"reduce_motion": "off"` in `RUN/data/config/settings.json`. Read its id from `tern ls`; the examples below use 5.
- A layout line per pane: `L() { "$V" tern "$RUN" ls | grep -E '^ +[0-9]+'; }`. A float shows `pip <corner> over <id>`, a hidden float `stashed <side>`.

- Split. Run `"$V" ctl "$RUN" key alt+n`. `L` shows a second tiled pane, focused, and pane 5 at half its width.
- Floating shell. Run `"$V" ctl "$RUN" key alt+f` in a tab with no float. `L` shows a new pane with `pip br over <tiled id>  focused`.
- Motion restore. Read `settings.json` and `RUN/data/config/plugin-data/zellij-keys/kv.json` right after the key. The setting is `"reduce_motion": "on"`, and `kv.json` holds `"reduce_motion_before_float": "off"`. Send no input or control commands for 1.5 s, then read those files again. The setting is `"off"`, and the saved key is gone. A hide on focus (`"$V" tern "$RUN" focus 5` from a float) gives the same result. `ctl focus` returns only after about 0.5 s, so use `tern focus` or a key for the immediate reading.
- Cascade. With three floats (Alt+F, then Alt+N twice), run `"$V" ctl "$RUN" dump '.tn-stage > .pip:not(.off)' | jq -c '[.elements[] | {rect, transform}]'` and `"$V" ctl "$RUN" dump .tn-stage | jq -c '.elements[0].rect'`. For stage x, y, w, h, drawn float k (from 0, in open order) has the rect x + 0.25 w + 16 k, y + 0.25 h + 32 (k mod 8), 0.5 w, 0.5 h, and the transform `none`. `"$V" ctl "$RUN" state | jq -c '[.pips[] | .mode]'` gives `["glance","glance","focus"]`. `dump .tn-pip-chip` gives no visible chip.
- Float moves. With one float (Alt+F), press `key ctrl+g`, `key m`, `key l`. The `.tn-stage > .pip:not(.off)` rect moves right by 0.1 w. Two more whole chords (Ctrl+G, M, L each) stop it at the right edge: its rect is x + 0.5 w - 10, the same y, w and h. Ctrl+G, R, H then makes it 0.1 w narrower with the same left edge, and `kv.json` holds a `dx` for it. Ctrl+G, R, `shift+=` and `alt+shift+=` (the + key) grow it by 0.1 w and 0.1 h, `-` and `alt+-` shrink it, and Ctrl+G, R, `=` gives the cascade rect again. `RUN/data/config/plugin-data/zellij-keys/kv.json` holds the spot under `float_spots`, by pane id, and loses it when the float closes or docks. After Alt+F twice (hide and show), a new float (Alt+N), `"$V" tern "$RUN" plugin reload` and `"$V" ctl "$RUN" resize 1100 760`, the moved float keeps its spot and snapped edges stay 10 px from the tab edges. In a second tab, a new float starts at the first cascade place.
- Float size limits. From the cascade place (Ctrl+G, R, `=`), three `alt+-` leave the float at 0.2 w and 0.2 h with the same center, x + 0.4 w and y + 0.4 h. A fourth `alt+-` changes nothing. Eight `alt+shift+=` fill the tab with 10 px on each side (five give only 0.7 w and 0.7 h), and a ninth changes nothing. One `alt+-` then gives x + 0.05 w, y + 0.05 h, 0.9 w, 0.9 h: a shrink lets go of the snapped edges.
- Many floats. Press Alt+N until the tab has 17 floats. `dump '.tn-stage > .pip'` shows 16 elements with `visible: true`, and `"$V" tern "$RUN" ls` shows them as `pip br`, `pip bl`, `pip tr` and `pip tl`. Then run `"$V" tern "$RUN" focus <oldest id>`. The `.pip-focus` element is visible, and 16 floats are still drawn. With the Go to picker open, 15 floats and the picker are drawn: count floats with `dump '.tn-stage > .pip:not(.off):not([aria-label^="Go to,"])'`, because the picker is a `.pip` too.
- Moves with 17 floats. If an earlier step resized the window, run `"$V" ctl "$RUN" resize 1280 800` first. Press Ctrl+G, M, H on the newest float. Its `.pip-focus` rect moves left and up into the tab because its cascade place hangs below it. The first horizontal step can differ by a few pixels from 0.1 w at a late cascade place. Record the moved rect. Then `tern focus` the oldest float, open the Go to picker with F1, and close it with Esc. After each step, exactly one drawn float has the moved rect.
- Hide floats. Run `"$V" ctl "$RUN" key alt+f` from a float, or `"$V" ctl "$RUN" focus left` to a tiled pane. Every `.pips[].mode` is `hidden`, and `L` shows up to 3 floats as `stashed right`. Later floats stash at their other corners. `dump .tn-pip-tab` shows visible native edge pills with icons and chevrons. Keep these pills visible. Alt+F from the tiled pane shows all floats and focuses the last used float.
- Native handle limitation. Move the second of two floats, hide both, then click its `.tn-pip-tab` handle by its `Show <title>` selector. On 0.9.7 it can preview at its cascade place while its sibling stays hidden and keyboard focus stays tiled. The stored spot remains unchanged; focusing it or Alt+F show-all restores the saved rect. Record this as the explicitly accepted [issue #8](https://github.com/daandden/tern-plugins/issues/8), not as passing saved-position preservation. Recheck after later Tern updates. No process-based observer is installed.
- Mixed state. With two floats hidden, run `"$V" tern "$RUN" focus <older float>`. `L` shows that float `focused` and the other one `stashed`. Then `"$V" tern "$RUN" focus <tiled id>` hides both: every `.pips[].mode` is `hidden`.
- Open from mixed state. Hide two floats, then native-focus the older one as above. Press Alt+N from it. All three floats are shown (`glance`, `glance`, `focus`); the float that was stashed is no longer hidden. Focus the tiled pane to hide all three.
- New tabs. After a hide in tab 1, run `"$V" ctl "$RUN" tab new` and press Alt+F: a float opens. `"$V" tern "$RUN" focus <tiled id>` hides it, Alt+F shows it with focus, and Alt+F again hides it. Do the same in two more new tabs. Each step gives the same modes in each tab.
- Tab round trip. Hide the floats with `focus left`, show them with Tern's own key (`"$V" ctl "$RUN" key ctrl+alt+cmd+p`) so the tiled pane keeps focus, then run `"$V" ctl "$RUN" tab new` and `tab 1`. Every `.pips[].mode` is still `glance`.
- Session round trip. With floats shown and a tiled pane still focused, run `"$V" ctl "$RUN" session new FloatFixture`, then `session switch Default`. The floats remain `glance`. This session belongs only to the isolated run.
- Close a float. With three floats shown, run `"$V" ctl "$RUN" close`. The float before the closed one has focus, and the others stay `glance`. After the last close, the tiled pane has focus.
- Picker. From a shown float, run `"$V" ctl "$RUN" plugins run plugin.goto.open`, then `key escape`. The float that opened it has focus, and the floats stay shown. Repeat with F1. For a jump in a split tab, open F1, press `ctrl+;`, and type `a` to show plain panes. Read `opts` from `goto.md` before each jump and choose the tiled pane that is not under the floats. Each jump hides the floats. Show the floats and repeat three times. Each new picker starts in agents-only view, so select all panes each time. With ten or more numbers, type both digits.
- Floating shell from a float. Focus a float, then run `"$V" ctl "$RUN" key alt+n`. `L` shows one more float over the same tiled pane.
- Tool float. Run `"$V" ctl "$RUN" key f2`. `L` shows `Yazi: <dir>` as a focused float, and its `.pip-focus` rect is x + 0.1 w, y + 0.1 h, 0.8 w, 0.8 h. `kv.json` holds `{"x":0.1,"y":0.1,"w":0.8,"h":0.8}` under `float_spots` for its pane id at once. F3 and F4 (`/opt/homebrew/bin/lazygit`, `/opt/homebrew/bin/btop`) open at the same rect, on top of the first. Ctrl+G, R, `=` moves the focused one to its cascade place and removes its spot. Quit a tool (`type q`), and its spot goes.
- Agent tab. Make `RUN/data/agent-sub`, `cd` the shell pane into it and check that `RUN/data/config/settings.json` has `"agent_placement": "new_tab"`. Press `"$V" ctl "$RUN" key alt+shift+t`. `ctl state` shows one more tab, active, and its focused pane runs omp in `agent-sub` (`.focused.cwd`). The shot shows the agent block and its composer. Close the tab with `"$V" ctl "$RUN" close`. Then focus the shell pane again, run `"$V" ctl "$RUN" palette '"New agent block"'` and `key enter`: one more tab again, not a split of the shell's tab.
- Scroll. Run `"$V" type "$RUN" 'seq 300'`. Read `"$V" ctl "$RUN" tree '.tn-pane.on:not(.off) .tv' | jq -r '.nodes[0].class'` (`tv`, or `tv scrolled` when the view is not at the bottom) and `"$V" ctl "$RUN" dump '.tn-pane.on:not(.off) .tv-thumb' | jq -c '.elements[0].rect'` (the scroll bar thumb). Ctrl+G, S, K gives `tv scrolled` and moves the thumb up a little, and Ctrl+G, S, J moves it back down by the same amount. Ctrl+G, S, Ctrl+B and Ctrl+G, S, `pageup` move it up much more (a page), and Ctrl+G, S, Ctrl+F and `pagedown` move it down a page. Ctrl+G, S, Ctrl+C gives `tv` and the thumb at its first place.
- Find. Press Ctrl+G, S, F. `"$V" ctl "$RUN" state | jq -c .find` is no longer `null`. Clear the bar with `key cmd+a` and `key backspace`, then run `"$V" ctl "$RUN" type 29`. `.find` gives `hits` above 1 and `current` equal to `hits` (the newest match). Ctrl+G, S, P lowers `current` by 1, and Ctrl+G, S, N raises it by 1. `key escape` closes the bar (`null`).
- Worktree fixture. Under `RUN/data`, make a bare `origin.git`, push a first commit, and clone it to `repo` and `other`. In `repo/.omp/config.yml`, set `worktree.base` to an absolute directory under `RUN/data`, such as `RUN/data/wt`. Add `.omp/` to the fixture repo's `.git/info/exclude` so the config does not make the checkout dirty. Run `git -C repo remote set-head origin -a`, switch `repo` to a new branch `feature`, and push a second commit to `master` from `other`.
- Worktree agent tab. In the shell pane, `cd` into `repo` and press `"$V" ctl "$RUN" key ctrl+shift+t`. `tree .toast` says `Updating master…`, and `tern ls` shows one new tab with omp in `RUN/data/wt/wt-<date>-<commit>`. Its HEAD is the second commit, and `repo` stays on `feature`. Repeat through the palette's `New agent tab in a new worktree`. Repeat from an existing fixture worktree and check that the main checkout still keeps its branch.
- Default branches. Repeat with `origin/HEAD` naming `main` and a nonstandard branch such as `trunk`. With `origin/HEAD`, adding the other local default branch does not change the chosen branch. Without an origin, exactly one local `main` or `master` opens a worktree. Both names or neither name give `No default branch` and create no tab.
- Worktree rejection. Each case leaves the worktrees unchanged and gives a toast whose classes include `toast` and `err`: an untracked file, a staged change, an unstaged change, a local commit on `master` (`1 unpushed commit`), diverged local and remote commits (`diverged`), and the shell pane in `RUN/data` (`Not in a git repo`). Wait for the toast to disappear before the next key.
- Failed fetch. An origin URL that does not exist gives an info toast and opens at cached `origin/master`. Remove the fixture's `refs/remotes/origin/master` and repeat to check the local `master` fallback. A failed fetch still rejects a known ahead or diverged local default branch.
- Fetch deadline. Use a loopback TCP listener that accepts a connection but sends no SSH banner, and set the fixture's origin to `ssh://git@127.0.0.1:<port>/fixture.git`, keeping its cached `origin/master`. Press Ctrl+Shift+T, then press it twice more while the fetch waits. After about 30 seconds (at most 31 seconds plus UI scheduling), the command shows `Could not reach origin; master may be old` and opens one agent tab. No extra tab opens from the busy presses. Stop the listener and remove the fixture's worktrees and agent sessions.
- Float and dock. With two tiled panes, focus one and press `"$V" ctl "$RUN" key ctrl+g`, then `key p`, then `key e`. `L` shows that pane as `pip <corner> over <other>`. The same keys again dock it, and it is tiled again.
- Break to a new tab. Press `key ctrl+g`, `key t`, `key b`. `"$V" ctl "$RUN" state | jq -c '[.tabs[] | {panes, active}]'` shows one more tab, active, with the pane.
- Break to the left. In the new tab, press `key ctrl+g`, `key t`, `key '['`. The tab list loses that tab, and the tab on the left has one more pane.
- Break to the right. Press `key ctrl+g`, `key t`, `key ']'`. The focused pane moves to the tab on the right. At the last tab, Tern creates a new tab.
- Focus. With three shown floats, exercise Alt+H, Alt+K, Alt+L, Alt+J, and Alt with each arrow. Focus moves backward or forward in open order and wraps. In a tiled pane, H and L move to that side's pane, then the adjacent tab when no pane is there. J and K move only within the tab.
- Palette. Repeat each named palette entry in the entry-point list, including all focus directions, all resize directions, both grow and shrink commands, both float toggle commands, all three tools, all three break commands, and New pane from both a tile and a float. Observe the same state or geometry change as with the corresponding key.
- Proof. Run `"$V" shot "$RUN" zk-<step>` after each layout change. The cascade shot shows the floats as half-tab boxes, each one a little lower and to the right of the one before it, with the focused one on top.

## Gotchas

- Ctrl+G, P, E does not float the only tiled pane of a tab. Split first.
- lazygit in `RUN/data`, which is not a git repo, asks to make one, and `q` does not quit it. Close its float with `"$V" ctl "$RUN" close`.
- A chord is one `key` call per key, and each step needs the whole chord: after Ctrl+G, M, L, a bare `l` types `l` into the pane. `key 'ctrl+g>p>e'` is not a key. Page Up and Page Down are `pageup` and `pagedown` in `ctl key`; `page_up` is no key.
- The find bar keeps its last query. Clear it before you type a new one.
- The agent-tab check runs omp in `agent-sub`, not directly in `RUN/data`. After `stop`, remove its session directory too (`~/.omp/agent/sessions/-<path of RUN/data/agent-sub under home, with / as ->`).
- The settings file changes back about 400 ms after the key, and Tern writes it a moment later. Read it 1.5 s after the key, not at 400 ms.
- A float command may schedule a second focus after its key returns. Before native-focusing a different pane for a mixed-state check, wait until `reduce_motion_before_float` disappears, then focus the pane and poll `.focused.id`. Otherwise a pending refocus can undo the test's setup.
- The plugin process inherits the Tern launcher's PATH, not the fish pane's PATH. A shell-launched run can find Homebrew while a Tern process launched with `/usr/bin:/bin:/usr/sbin:/sbin` cannot find bare `omp`. Keep the absolute Homebrew paths.
- `stop` deletes worktrees placed under `RUN/data`, but nested agent session directories remain under `~/.omp/agent/sessions/`. After stopping, remove only directories whose encoded path starts with this run's `RUN/data` path.
- `.tabs[].panes` in `ctl state` counts tiled panes only. Count floats from the `pip` lines of `L`.
- A float that the mouse focuses still animates. The motion check covers key and palette paths only.
- `ctl state` gives `glance` for a shown float without focus. `glance` is not a corner preview here: the `dump` box tells. A hidden float is `hidden`.
- goto closes the picker before it focuses a jump target, and Tern first moves focus to the tiled pane under the picker. So a jump to that tiled pane, or to the tab row of a one-pane tab, acts like Esc and goes back to the float.
- `.pips[].deck` in `ctl state` is `whole`, `peek1` to `peek3` or `hidden`, and `hidden` means that Tern does not draw the float. `"$V" ctl "$RUN" pip fan '"#1"'` fans the stack of the oldest float as if the pointer were over it, and Tern then draws every float of that stack. Count drawn floats before any `pip fan`, because the fan stays until a fan of another stack.
- The stage keeps the panes of each tab that you looked at, with `off`. Select drawn floats with `.tn-stage > .pip:not(.off)`, or a dump also lists the floats of other tabs with an empty rect.
- `"$V" tern "$RUN" ls` lists floats in no fixed order, and its pane sizes lag behind the window. Find the oldest float by the lowest pane id, and measure tiled panes with `dump '.tn-stage > .tn-pane:not(.pip)'`.
- A key binding written into `RUN/data/config/settings.json` while the window runs does not take effect, and Tern writes the file back. `start` copies `~/.config/tern/settings.json`, so new keys need to be there before `start`. Your own Tern rewrites `~/.config/tern/settings.json` in the same way, so a hand edit there is lost too. If a key check fails for every key of one kind, look for its binding in the copied file first.
